### wARRR Discontinuation

Support for the wARRR tokens on BSC and Ethereum, bridged through Aureus Trading, is discontinued following the [security incident](https://polarity.exchange/blog/2023/03/security-incident-conclusive-statement/) on February 17, 2023. [[link]](https://polarity.exchange/blog/2023/11/important-announcement-gateway-closure/)

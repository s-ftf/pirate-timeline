### Spending After One Confirmation

The unified wallet v1.1.2 beta reduces its spending confirmation requirement from ten blocks to one, letting users spend received ARRR sooner. [[link]](https://github.com/PirateNetwork/Stashi-Wallet/releases/tag/v1.1.2)
